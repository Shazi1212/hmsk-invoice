E-invoices (XRechnung)

"XRechnung (XML)" exports an issued invoice, credit note or self-billing invoice as XRechnung 3.0 in the UN/CEFACT CII syntax (EN 16931). It needs the sender's email, phone and IBAN and the client's email. For public-sector clients, enter their Leitweg-ID in the client record; otherwise the buyer reference is sent as "n/a". The XML is the legal invoice when you send it as an e-invoice; the PDF is a human-readable copy.

About the compliance checks

The checks follow § 14 / § 14a / § 19 UStG and § 35a GmbHG (Germany), Art.º 36.º CIVA and the certified-software rule (Portugal) and the EU VAT Directive 2006/112/EC (Art. 196, 226). They catch missing mandatory data and the wrong VAT treatment; they don't replace the tax adviser for special cases (construction § 13b, OSS, margin schemes, turnover limits). This tool is not tax advice.
